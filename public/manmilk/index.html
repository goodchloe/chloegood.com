The Problem

For thousands of years, men have been told to provide. Never to produce.

Modern masculinity taught men to give money, protection and advice. It never taught them to give from the body.

The result is a generation of men who feel cut off from their own capacity to nurture. They hover beside the feeding, carry the diaper bag, ask "is there anything I can do?" and mean it.

There is something you can do. It just takes 12 weeks and a willingness to soften.

The Method

The Four Streams™

Our proprietary curriculum blends attachment science, breathwork and ancestral wisdom into a path from guarded to generous.

I

Thaw

Weeks 1–3. We locate decades of stored self-sufficiency in the chest and invite it to melt. Expect tears. Tears are a precursor fluid.

II

Attune

Weeks 4–6. Daily partnered presence practice. You'll learn to sense hunger in others before they name it, and to stop solving it with a sandwich.

III

Summon

Weeks 7–9. Guided visualization, warm compresses and the Ancestral Lullaby Circle. Many men report a tingling. We call this "the Knock."

IV

Flow

Weeks 10–12. Integration, ritual and your Letdown Ceremony, held at a private retreat center with oat-milk lattes, out of respect.

Questions

Frequently asked, gently answered.

Is this biologically possible?

Men have mammary tissue, milk ducts and the hormone receptors to go with them. The rest is a conversation between the body and a culture that never asked it to try. We are reopening that conversation.

What if nothing comes out?

Then you'll have spent 12 weeks learning to be present with someone else's needs without fixing them. Most of our partners consider this the more impressive outcome.

Do I need to be a father?

No. Uncles, mentors, godfathers and men who simply sense they have more to give are all welcome.

Is there a refund policy?

We offer a full refund to any man who completes all 12 weeks and still believes breastfeeding is "basically free."

Will this make me less of a man?

It will make you more of a mammal.
